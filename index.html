GOT QUESTIONS?

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Here are answers to common questions about visiting our dental clinic.

Still need help?Contact our team
How can I book a dental appointment?

You can submit the appointment request form on this page or contact the clinic directly by phone. Your visit is not confirmed until the clinic responds.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any relevant dental records, a list of current medications, and details of any allergies or health concerns that may affect your care.

How often should I visit a dentist?

Many people benefit from regular dental checkups, but the recommended interval depends on your oral health and your dentist's advice.

Do you offer cosmetic dental treatments?

Cosmetic options may include professional whitening and other treatments. A dental consultation can help determine which options are suitable for you.

What if I feel nervous about dental treatment?

Tell the dental team about your concerns before treatment. They can explain the process and discuss appropriate ways to help you feel more comfortable.
